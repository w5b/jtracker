            Where do I find my session ID?
            1. Sign in at j7tracker.io in your browser.
            2. Open the developer tools, then Application, then Local storage, then https://j7tracker.io.
            3. Copy the value next to sessionId and paste it above.

            It goes only to the local server on this computer, and the page never shows it again. Remembered sessions are saved in ~/.jtracker/session, readable only by your user.
